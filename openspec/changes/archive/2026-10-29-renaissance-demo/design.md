## Context

The code for the renaissance look is already on the `renaissance-style` branch; this change brings the README and the specs up to it. Section 1 is what the course grades as the demo description: three to six sentences, present tense, real filenames, real screens, a real number, shown on 1 or 8 December as screenshots in a five-minute presentation.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A section 1 that every screenshot of the presentation can prove, sentence by sentence.
- Keep the rest of the README true for both looks.

**Non-Goals:**
- No code change. The original `index.html`, `css/` and `js/` stay as they are.
- Section 2 (the shape) and section 5 (risks) do not change: the concept is the same.

## Decisions

- **The demo runs on a dry, mild day: 16°, overcast, rain 6%.** On a rainy day only one pair of shoes in the demo closet is rain-proof, so re-spinning the shoes would fall back to a pair that is not; a dry day shows the re-spin honestly. Alternative: keep the old 14° light rain, rejected for that reason.
- **The model in the story is Botticelli's Flora.** The renaissance page shows painted women instead of the stock photos, and Flora is its first. If I upload my own photo before the screenshots, the sentence names that instead.
- **The re-spun piece in the story is the shoes, "in the laundry".** It is the clearest reason to change one piece and keep the rest.
- **The date in the status bar is the day the screenshots are taken.** The old text promised `2026-11-30`; the sentence names the date only through the screenshot, so it cannot be wrong on the day.
- **Why a second look gets its own short paragraph, outside section 1.** Section 1 must stay at five sentences; the reason (the first look never felt like mine, painting motivates me to use it) goes under it as "Why a second look".

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The forecast on the screenshot day is not 16° and dry] → take the screenshots on a day that matches, or change the numbers in section 1 to the day's real forecast before 7 November.
- [The daily quote on the screenshot day differs from the one named] → section 1 does not quote it word for word; it says the muse shows the day's quote.
