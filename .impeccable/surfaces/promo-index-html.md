# Surface brief: promo page (`promo/index.html`)

Scope: one Persuade page that says what Ms. Match is for, who it is for and how to try it, and sends the visitor to the live atelier. Full brief: `docs/designs/promo-page-brief.md`; copy: `docs/designs/promo-page-content.md`; product truth: `PRODUCT.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The morning is a book of hours. The page refuses the landing-page default of hero, feature cards and logos: it reads as one illuminated folio, five hours of a real morning in the atelier, and the reading position is the hour.

OWN-WORLD: The renaissance atelier's own materials: linen vellum ground inside a gilded border, umber ink, oxblood rubrics, gold leaf only as frame and rule, never as text fill. Cormorant Garamond for the text hand, Cinzel for rubrics and hours, Manrope for small print. Miniatures are real screenshots in arch-topped gilt frames.

STORY: The visitor sees Flora dressed for the day's forecast, reads that the weather picks the outfit from your own closet, follows five hours of one real morning (12 November 2026, Budapest, 8° and rain), learns who it is for and that photos stay in the browser, and clicks Try it now.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A margin calendar of the five hours at left. In the main column, an arch-topped miniature of the atelier on arrival, about 760 wide; under it the oxblood rubric caption; then an illuminated initial L in an oxblood square opening the headline, one line on what it does and for whom, and Try it now in oxblood beside See the code, all above the fold at 1440 by 900.

FORM: Book of hours, position 2 on the ordered list. seed:d386e07b7a0706a7. Signature interaction: the margin calendar is sticky and marks the hour being read with a moving gold rule.
