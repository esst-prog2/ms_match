# Process: the promo page

One row per step of the promo page homework: the step, what the tool did, and what I decided.

| Step | What the tool did | What I decided |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| 1. The working rule (2026-11-12) | Claude Code opened the `promo-page` branch, created this file, added the rule to AGENTS.md and logged the decisions in PLANNING_LOG.md. | After each step: a commit and a row here. The rule also goes into AGENTS.md, so it survives a new session. The homework lives on its own branch, `promo-page`, and is merged into `main` before publishing. |
| 2. The content, with gstack `/office-hours` (2026-11-12) | Claude Code installed gstack (with Bun) outside the repo. `/office-hours` asked me one question at a time: audience, opening, how to try it, first sentence. It searched the web with generic terms and found that Stylebook also keeps photos on the device. It proposed five premises and three structures, then wrote the content as a design doc. An independent reviewer checked the doc against the code in two rounds (32 findings). It caught that Match me is random apart from a rain filter, and that every esst-prog2 Pages site shares one origin, so they share storage and a location permission. Result: [`docs/designs/promo-page-content.md`](docs/designs/promo-page-content.md). | Builder mode, since this is a course project. The page is for people like me. It opens on Flora dressed for the day, its first sentence says the weather decides, and **Try it now** opens the live renaissance page; the promo page lives in `promo/`. Structure B, "A morning in the atelier". I agreed to all five premises and skipped the second opinion. I approved the copy with the reviewer's minor fixes and kept the full doc in the repo. Still open: what Pages serves (decided before step 4), and whether Match me should follow warmth too. |
| 3a. The look, with Impeccable: shape (2026-11-12) | Claude Code installed Impeccable outside the repo. Impeccable first ran `init`, asked me three questions and wrote `PRODUCT.md`. In `shape` it asked about the look, the images and what would make the page wrong. It then derived seven page structures from the content inside the renaissance world, rolled dice to deal three of them, and showed them as sketches on a decision page in my browser. It wrote the design brief from the one I picked; no code yet. Result: [`docs/designs/promo-page-brief.md`](docs/designs/promo-page-brief.md). | Installed globally, without its design hook, with telemetry off. The promo page keeps the renaissance world and shows real screenshots. It must not make an untrue claim or look like a generic landing page. Of the catalogue, the salon hang and the book of hours, I chose the book of hours: the morning as five hours, Flora as the opening miniature. Fonts come from Google Fonts. I approved the brief on one condition: my existing pages must not be harmed. |
| 3b. The look, with Impeccable: generate (2026-11-12) | Claude Code built the page from the brief. It took six screenshots of the live atelier with gstack's headless browser, in a profile with empty storage, so nothing of mine could appear. Impeccable `generate` then put three layouts of the opening into my own browser: the headline first, the text on a cartouche over the picture, and a manuscript page. Each had sliders for density and placement. After I picked one, it wrote that one into `promo/index.html` and `promo/style.css` and removed all of its temporary code. Its detector found nothing to fix, and my existing pages stayed unchanged. | Screenshots by Claude, of the real morning of 12 November (8° and rain), so the copy follows that day. Of the three layouts I chose the manuscript page: one block of text with Flora's arched miniature set into its right side, at the default density. |
| 3c. The look, with Impeccable: critique (2026-11-12) | Impeccable `critique` ran two separate reviews that could not see each other. The first was a design review through six eyes: a classmate, my teacher, a stranger, a first-time visitor, a stress tester and a phone user. The second was its detector: nothing in the source, plus 5 typography findings in the browser on desktop and 7 on a phone. Score: 23/32 (Good). It named the weakest part: **Try it now** opened the renaissance page's own front page instead of the atelier. Flora and Match me were 1,144px further down, and the advice about the location prompt was far down the promo page. Report: [`docs/designs/promo-page-critique.md`](docs/designs/promo-page-critique.md). | Fix the weakest part and the quick typography now. Try it now goes straight to the atelier (`../renaissance/#atelier`), and the hero says that "no" to location is fine. No lone last words, shorter lines, and no breaks at hyphens. Left for later: the images of the last hours, the phone's row of hours, the name's presence, and the lower third. |
